Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Amazon Fresh business performance, customer behavior, and product insights.
Created KPI cards and interactive visualizations to track sales, customers, orders, products, and Prime membership.
Analyzed customer demographics, membership distribution, spending patterns, and city-wise customer activity.
Evaluated product prices, ratings, reviews, and category-wise performance to identify product trends and customer feedback.
Implemented filters, bookmarks, and tooltips to provide an interactive and user-friendly dashboard for better data-driven decision-making.
